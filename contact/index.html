Good reasons to write:

Things this site cannot help with:

  • Problems with a gambling account, withdrawal or bonus — this site is not an operator and has no access to any account. Contact the operator; the operator's terms name an independent dispute body if that fails.
  • Requests to link to, review or promote a gambling site. The site carries no commercial links.
  • Recruitment or agency enquiries. The site is personal and not for hire.
