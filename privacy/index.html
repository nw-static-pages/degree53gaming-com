Privacy

This site collects the minimum. No accounts, no newsletter, no advertising, no tracking pixels from third parties.

  • Server logs. The web server records IP address, time, page and browser type for security and diagnostics; logs rotate and are deleted automatically after a short period.
  • Email. Messages sent to the contact address are kept for as long as needed to reply and to record corrections.

No cookies are set by the site itself and no analytics script runs on it. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers or gambling operators.

Under UK GDPR you may ask what is held about you, request correction or deletion, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Requests go to the address on the contact page.
